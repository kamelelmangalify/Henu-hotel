Welcome to HENU

Nestled in the historic Nazlet El-Semman district of Giza, HENU Hotel offers a unique blend of modern luxury and timeless Egyptian heritage. Wake up to the awe-inspiring sight of the Great Pyramids right outside your window.

Our boutique property features pharaonic-themed decor, comfortable accommodations, and exceptional service to ensure your stay in Egypt is truly unforgettable. Whether you are here for adventure or relaxation, HENU is your perfect home base.
